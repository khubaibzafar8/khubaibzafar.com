About

Growing ideas. Advancing sustainability. Empowering people.

Khubaib Zafar is a Pakistani horticulturist, sustainability advocate, entrepreneur, public speaker and education consultant. His work connects horticultural sciences with practical approaches to climate resilience, urban greening, youth engagement, research and international education.

He holds an MSc (Hons.) in Horticultural Sciences from the University of Agriculture Faisalabad (CGPA 3.38/4.00) and a BSc (Hons.) in Agriculture from the University of Sargodha (CGPA 3.37/4.00).

He is Founder & CEO of KB Hortica, Founder of Climate Change Combat (C³), Co-Founder & Vice President of Pakistan Ideological Civilization, Founding Chairman of Jhang Youth Alliance, and Study Abroad Consultant at The EduWisers.
